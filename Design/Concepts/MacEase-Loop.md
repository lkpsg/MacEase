## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE original, sophisticated macOS app icon for MacEase. The user's aesthetic references are the distinctive abstract brand symbols of Codex/OpenAI, Claude and Qoder. Study the design principles: a confident standalone symbol, disciplined negative space, a compact memorable silhouette, restrained near-monochrome palette. Make an original design, NOT a copy, remix or close derivative of any of those logos. This is a fresh design; ignore every earlier MacEase icon.

Primary artwork: an original compact abstract loop made of THREE broad, smoothly curved interlocking segments arranged in an asymmetrical rounded triangular composition. The three segments should feel like one coherent, calm flowing shape. One generous softly triangular central opening, clear breathing gaps where segments pass each other. Charcoal near-black solid ink. Think a beautiful bespoke identity mark for an independent productivity tool. Strong silhouette at 32 pixels, no small cuts or tiny details. The geometry must feel carefully designed, not like a generic swirl. Keep the three-part structure visually simple and readable. Do not draw the six-part OpenAI knot, a starburst, a command symbol, infinity symbol, arrow, keyboard, keycap, literal letter, paper sheet, gear, sparkle, checkmark, cursor, mascot or any functional UI pictogram.

Composition: centered large charcoal mark, occupying about 59% of the 1024x1024 canvas, on a warm off-white rounded-square macOS tile occupying about 82% of the canvas with even transparent outer margins. Flat vector-like artwork, absolutely no gradients, bevels, embossing, 3D material, glow, shadow, texture or animation. The off-white tile and charcoal mark are the only two colours. Actual alpha transparency outside the tile. Front-on view. One finished icon only, no labels, lettering, grid, alternatives, presentation board or watermark. Prefer clear intentional form and aesthetic restraint over adding meaning or decoration.
